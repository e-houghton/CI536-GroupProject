User Agreement

HandyCraftz is a marketplace were you as a user can both buy and sell handmade items.

However this comes with considerations. We expect users to compile with this policy. This covers: what can be sold, explicit content on the page, labour, etc.

Prohibited Items and Content

Items that cannot be sold on the HandyCraftz marketplace include:

  • illegal items (drugs, weapons, stolen items)
  • Dangerous items (explosives, blades, hazardous chemicals, and other potential harmful products)
  • Food Items and Animals
Any breach of this policy will be met with account termination.

Explicit Content Policy

On the HandyCraftz marketplace we aim to create a space for all ages, with this aim we prohibit any explicit content.

  • Product listings (images, descriptions, etc) should not include sexually graphic content, violent content, or hate speech.
  • Profile images should not content sexually suggestive or graphic content.
  • Users should not solicit of explicit content or engage in harassment when communicating.
Any breach of this policy will be met with account termination.

Labour and Seller Standards

  • All sellers must be at least 18 years old or the legal adult age in there country.
  • Any labour performed to create items listed on HandyCraftz cannot be made at any point by a child
  • Sellers must compile with any labour laws in there country
Any breach of this policy will be met with account termination.

Intellectual Property

  • HandyCraftz will remove listing the infringe on intellectual property
  • all items listed must be original or the interllectual property must be owned my the seller

User Conduct

  • Description of items must accurately represent them.
  • Any transactions made for the sale or purchase of items listed should made through HandyCraftz
  • No fake reviews or ratings on product listings or accounts.
  • Communication should be respectful. harassment, threats, or discriminatory behaviour is prohibited
