Privacy Policy

At HandyCraftz, we take the privacy of our users into consideration at every step. We strive to maintain transparency on how we collect, use, share, and retain information throughout.

This policy allies to anyone using our services with or without an account. This can include buyers, sellers, or guests who have made a purchase on HandyCraftz.

This policy does not apply to the practices of HandyCraftz sellers or any third parties that you may share information with. You should understand the privacy policies of any third party that you share information with.

1. What information we collect.

We collect infomation neede for accound infomation and shipping. This incudes contact infomation such as a email address and phone number, and shipping infomation such as a post code and address.

We also collect miscellaneous infomation for user accounts such as a name for the account and optionally a profile picture.

2. What we collect information for.

We collect infomation in order to provide basic functionality for HandyCraftz.

Contact infomation is collected in order to facilitate updates on orders and verification of accounts.

Shipping infomation is collected in order for packages to be shipped to the correct location.

3. What infomation we share.

We share no infomation with any third parties with the exeption of shipping infomation, this is shared with the currier used to deliver the package.

All infomation is stored in brighton domains, the university of brighton servers.

4. What infomation we retain and how long we keep it.

We retain all acound and shipping infomation form account creation up untill the deletion of that accound or any subject access requests.
